## Interpretation of the original run

The trial has one replicate per arm. At the final checkpoint, charter selected a mean 986 tests
from 1,989; AnaxiGraph selected 985 from 1,536. Percentages alone do not rank their architecture.
AnaxiGraph coding plus analysis took about 49% longer. Observed test-selection recall was 0.871;
zero misses among sampled mutants does not establish complete consumer assurance. New-integration
probes showed useful isolation in both charter and AnaxiGraph arms. New qualification must identify
what the tool adds beyond those shared instructions.
